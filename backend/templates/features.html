Features

Everything You Need to Generate, Take, and Grade Exams

auto_awesome

AI Exam Generation

Upload your own syllabus or study material — or just describe a topic — and UstadExam generates a full exam paper from it, with control over difficulty so the same source material can produce an easier practice set or a harder mock exam depending on what you need.

fact_check

Automatic AI Grading

Every subjective answer is graded automatically with a "why this score" breakdown, not just a number — content correctness and grammar are scored and deducted separately, and each answer is compared against an ideal model answer so you can see exactly what was missing.

history

Exam History Dashboard

Every exam you generate and every attempt you grade is saved to your account, with scores tracked over time — so progress (or a slipping trend) is visible at a glance instead of scattered across one-off paper printouts.

target

Weak-Topic Detection

UstadExam flags any topic averaging below roughly 60% across your graded attempts, so instead of re-reading an entire syllabus before the next exam, you know precisely which topics actually need review.

timer

Timed Practice Mode

Exams run on a real clock with auto-submit when time runs out, so practice actually simulates exam conditions instead of open-ended, unlimited-time question answering.

verified_user

Integrity Logging for Teachers

For teachers who need a bit more confidence in remote attempts, UstadExam quietly logs tab-switch activity and flags unusually fast submissions on each attempt — a trust signal alongside the grade, not a replacement for it.
