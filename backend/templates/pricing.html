Pricing FAQ

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes — you can upgrade to Student Pro or Teacher Pro whenever you like. Downgrading takes effect at the end of your current paid period, so you keep Pro access you've already paid for.

Is there a free trial for Pro?

There's no separate Pro trial right now — the Free plan itself is the way to try UstadExam before upgrading. Flagging for confirmation: if you'd rather offer a time-limited Pro trial, let me know and I'll update this answer and the actual upgrade flow to match.

What payment methods are supported?

JazzCash and EasyPaisa. Upgrades are confirmed manually today — pay via either method, then send your payment confirmation over WhatsApp and your account is upgraded once it's verified.
