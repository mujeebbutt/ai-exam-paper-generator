Privacy Policy

⚠️ Placeholder text for development purposes. Have a lawyer review this before real launch — especially given a student user base that may include minors, which can trigger additional obligations (e.g. COPPA in the US, GDPR-K in the EU/UK) beyond what's drafted here.

Information We Collect

Account details you provide at registration (name, email, role, grade or subject), content you upload for exam generation, and exam attempt data (answers, scores, and integrity signals such as tab-switch counts).

How We Use It

To generate and grade exams, show you your own progress and history, and let teachers review integrity signals for exams they own. We do not sell personal data.

Third-Party Processing

Uploaded study material and student answers are sent to Google's Gemini API for question generation and grading, subject to Google's own data handling terms.

Children's Privacy

If this platform is used by students under 18, parental/guardian consent and additional safeguards may be legally required depending on jurisdiction. This has not yet been implemented and must be addressed before real-world launch.

Data Retention

Account and exam data is retained until you request deletion. Contact your administrator to request account or data removal.

Contact

Questions about this policy should be directed to the platform administrator.
